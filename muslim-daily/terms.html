Muslim Daily

What this app is

Muslim Daily is a free mobile app providing the Quran, prayer times, the Hijri calendar, the 99 Names of Allah, du'as and adhkar for daily Islamic practice. It is offered "as is," without warranty of any kind. The developer is a sole trader registered in Portugal.

License

You may use the app freely on devices you own. You may not redistribute the bundled religious texts in violation of their original sources' licenses, including the Tanzil project's attribution requirement for the Quran text.

Religious content

Prayer times are computed from accepted algorithms and the Umm al-Qura Hijri calendar. We make every effort to be accurate, but local moonsighting, high-latitude rules and differing scholarly conventions can produce small variations. The app is a tool, not a religious authority — when in doubt, consult your local masjid or a scholar.
