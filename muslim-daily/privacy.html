Privacy Policy

Muslim Daily

We collect nothing.

Muslim Daily is built on the principle that an app for daily Islamic practice should not surveil its users. The app has no user accounts, sends nothing to a remote server, and includes no analytics, no advertising, no trackers and no crash-reporting SDKs.

What stays on your device

The following is stored locally — on your device only — and never leaves it: your selected language; your calculation method and Asr school; your last known coordinates and city name; your prayer-reminder preferences; your tasbih counter; Quran bookmarks and last-read position; names you add to your du'a list; and whether you have completed the welcome flow. Deleting the app removes all of it.

Permissions

Location (when in use) — used to compute prayer times and the direction of Qibla. Coordinates are stored on your device and never transmitted.

Notifications — if you enable prayer-time reminders. All notifications are scheduled and fired locally by your device; there is no server and no remote push.

The Sadaqah donation

If you choose to offer Sadaqah, the app opens a Stripe-hosted payment page in the in-app browser. Once you leave the app for that page, Stripe's privacy policy applies to your interaction with their payment form. We never see your payment details.

Children

The app is rated 4+ and suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect data about children — or anyone else.
