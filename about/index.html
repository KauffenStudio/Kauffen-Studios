About

A boutique studio, built on craft.

Kauffen Studios is a small web design & development studio based in Portugal, working with ambitious brands around the world.

We design and build websites the way we'd want our own built — custom from the ground up, fast by default, and made to last beyond a launch-day screenshot.

Founded by Augustin Agapii, the studio stays deliberately small. That means the person you talk to is the person doing the work, and every project gets genuine attention rather than being passed down a production line.

We've designed and built sites across SaaS, eCommerce, local services, portfolios and the creative industries — from a travel eSIM storefront to a contractor's locally-optimised site. You can see a selection of them in our work.

What we believe

01

Craft over templates

Every site is designed and built from scratch around your brand. No themes, no page-builders, no compromise.

02

Performance is a feature

Fast sites rank better, convert better and feel better. We treat speed as a design constraint, not an afterthought.

03

Small by design

We take on a handful of projects at a time so each one gets real attention from the people actually doing the work.

04

Honest communication

Clear scope, clear pricing, clear timelines. No jargon, no surprises — just straight answers.
