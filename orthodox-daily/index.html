A free, ad-free Orthodox prayer book and liturgical calendar — designed to disappear into your daily rule, not compete with it.

  • Morning & Evening Rules
  • The Hours — First, Third, Sixth, Ninth
  • Small Compline
  • Trisagion Prayers
  • Pre-Communion preparation
  • Thanksgiving after Communion
  • Three Canons
  • Four Akathists
  • Daily saints & feasts
  • Daily Scripture readings
  • Fasting rules for the day
  • 20 years of liturgical calendar

Five languages — English, Russian, Greek, Romanian, Serbian.
Julian (Old Style) and Revised Julian calendars.
